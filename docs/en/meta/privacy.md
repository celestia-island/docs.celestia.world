# Privacy Policy

This policy describes what data the celestia-island services collect during the closed beta and how it is used.

## What we collect

- **Account data** — email address and account metadata needed to operate your account.
- **Usage records** — API and service usage data, used for metering and billing during and after the beta.
- **Logs** — request and service logs generated in the normal course of operation.

## How we use it

- **Service operation** — running the services, authentication, and support.
- **Abuse prevention** — detecting and responding to abuse or attacks.
- **Usage metering** — measuring usage for internal billing and capacity planning.

## Retention

Data is retained only as long as needed for the purposes above. Logs are typically retained for 30 days; account and usage records are retained for the lifetime of the account plus any period required by applicable law.

## What we do not do

- We do not **sell** your data.
- We do not collect or process payment card data; any future payment processing is handled by third-party providers under their own policies.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue in the relevant [celestia-island](https://github.com/celestia-island) repository.
